Diagrams Claude draws, in a file you keep.

Sabura is a whiteboard for Claude. Ask for a diagram, change it right in the chat, and save it as one HTML file that anyone can open, edit and pass on. No account, nothing to install, works offline.

Add Sabura to Claude Or start a blank board

What you get

One file, editor included
Save Copy writes the board and the editor into a single HTML file. Open it years from now and it still works, with no service behind it.
Edits Claude can see
Move a box or rename a label in the chat, and Claude picks up what you changed before it adds anything else.
Opens anywhere
Any modern browser, on any computer, offline. The person you send it to needs no account and no app.
Nothing kept on our side
The Sabura connector builds a board and forgets it. Your boards live in your chats and your files. Privacy details

Add Sabura to Claude

  1. Open your connectors in Claude In Claude, go to Settings, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Paste the Sabura address Name it Sabura and paste this address. Sabura needs no sign-in.
  3. Ask for a board Try “Draw our sign-up flow on a Sabura board.” The board opens in the chat, ready to edit.

Don’t see Add custom connector? Your Claude plan or your organization may not allow custom connectors. The docs cover this and other common problems.
