Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026

This policy covers the Sabura plugin for Claude, the Sabura editor, and this website. In short: Sabura has no server that sees your boards, no accounts, and no cookies, analytics or advertising.

The Sabura plugin

The plugin is a set of instructions for Claude, the Sabura editor, and a script Claude runs to build boards. The script runs where Claude runs code: in Claude’s own environment on claude.ai and in the Claude app, or on your computer in Claude Code. It reads and writes board files there and makes no network requests. Nothing about your boards or your use of Sabura is sent to Sabura.

The Sabura editor and saved files

The editor runs entirely in your browser or in the chat’s file preview. Board files make no network requests when opened. The editor remembers one setting in your browser, the light or dark interface theme, and it is never sent anywhere.

The former Sabura connector

Until 23 November 2026, Sabura was also offered as a connector at sabura-connector.fly.dev. That service built boards in memory and kept no board content. It has been shut down and its server deleted. Fly.io, which hosted it, may keep platform logs for a limited time under its privacy policy.

This website

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as visitors’ IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

Services involved

ServiceRole

Sabura doesn’t collect, sell or share data.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new effective date at the top.
